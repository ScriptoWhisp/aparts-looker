Card padding 16. Gap between cards 12. Page gutter 24. Radius 8 (4 on chips, 14 on the hero).
Icons & motion
Phosphor, 16px, weight “regular”, currentColor only — one library, no emoji in the UI chrome. Score, status and flags carry meaning through colour + shape, never through an emoji.
120 ms opacity, 160 ms transform, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1). Accordions animate height only. No bounce, no spring, nothing loops. prefers-reduced-motion kills all of it.
